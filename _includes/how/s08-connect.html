08 · engine

Connect the services

After step 07, every repository is summarized on its own, and each call into another service is still a gap. The engine fills a gap by copying the handler's summary onto the contract, renumbered to match the caller's arguments. The caller then reads it like any other summary: no new algorithm. Whoever learns something is summarized again, which can change a contract it serves in turn, so the engine repeats until nothing changes.
