06 · engine

Load every repository as one program

The engine loads the facts of all three repositories into one program. A call into another service is still a loose end: it names a contract, and nothing connects it to the handler on the other side yet. Next to the facts, the engine reads the catalog: the security policy, as data. It names the calls that bring untrusted data in (sources), the dangerous ones (sinks), and the ones that make data safe (sanitizers).
