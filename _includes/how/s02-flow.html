02 · frontends

Map how data moves inside each function

For every function, the frontend follows each value from where it comes in (a parameter, the receiver, what a call returns) to where it goes out (a return, an argument of a call). The result is a small map. One rule matters more than any other: no line crosses a call. What the callee does with its inputs is not known yet, so the map stops at the call's inputs and starts again at its outputs. The engine fills that gap later, and a call to another service is just one more gap.
