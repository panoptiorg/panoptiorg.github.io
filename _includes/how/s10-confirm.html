Check it backwards

With --backward, the engine checks every finding a second time, from the other end. It starts at the sink and follows the data back through the real maps of each function, not the summaries, keeping track of exactly which value and field it needs. If it reaches a source, the finding is confirmed. If it runs out of places to look, it is refuted. If it meets something it cannot model, it stays undecided.
